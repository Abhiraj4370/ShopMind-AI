# ShopMind v16 — Decision Commerce Upgrade

## Results experience
- AI shopping command bar with Best Overall / Lowest Price / Top Rated quick modes.
- Collapsible smart filters instead of a permanently dense filter row.
- Grid/List view toggle.
- Product compare selection (up to 3 offers) with a sticky comparison tray.
- Stronger product-card hierarchy for price, ETA, merchant, stock, score and actions.
- Merchant comparison rows include product thumbnails.

## Product details
- Sticky product media on desktop.
- Trust/location/price-tracking signal row.
- Compact verdict cards for ShopMind score, price signal and delivery.
- Responsive single-column mobile details layout.

## Design principle
ShopMind should feel like a decision layer on top of shopping sources: the UI prioritizes the decision (best overall, cheapest, fastest, trusted) instead of only showing a catalog.

## Runtime note
This environment did not complete `npm ci`, so a full Vite production build could not be executed here. Source-level delimiter/import checks were performed. No retailer pricing or inventory was fabricated.
