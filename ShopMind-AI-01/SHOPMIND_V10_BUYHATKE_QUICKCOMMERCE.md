# ShopMind AI v10 — BuyHatke-style Deal Intelligence + Instant Commerce

## UX upgrades
- Search modes: AI Shop, Compare, Instant Delivery, and Paste Product Link.
- BuyHatke-inspired deal intelligence: multi-store comparison, rolling price history, price signals, price-drop alerts, wishlist, and transparent AI score.
- Product detail view now includes price intelligence, current-vs-history context, review sentiment signals, delivery indicator, save/alert actions, and merchant link.
- Instant Delivery keeps quick-commerce sources separate from standard e-commerce and adds manual pincode input for better regional matching.
- Deal Radar explains the major shopping-intelligence features on the home page instead of hiding them inside result screens.

## Important accuracy boundaries
- ShopMind does not invent coupons, exact ETAs, inventory, or live prices.
- Instant-delivery windows remain indicative unless a source explicitly supplies live data.
- Price history is built from prices captured during ShopMind searches; sparse history is shown honestly.
- Real retailer checkout and coupon automation still require official retailer/affiliate APIs or browser-extension/app integrations.
