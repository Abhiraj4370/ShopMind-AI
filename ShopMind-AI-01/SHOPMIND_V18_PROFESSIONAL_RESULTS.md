# ShopMind v18 — Professional Decision Commerce

## What changed
- Professional results workspace with a persistent desktop smart-filter rail.
- Best Overall / Lowest Price / Top Rated decision tabs remain available.
- Grid/List views retained with responsive behavior.
- Compare tray supports up to three selected offers and jumps to merchant comparison.
- Product image badges show value/quick-delivery context and ShopMind score.
- Product detail score normalization fixed for both 0–1 and 0–100 backend formats.
- Filter reset and mobile responsive filter layout added.

## Product philosophy
Search → understand → filter → compare → inspect → buy.

No fake live price, inventory, ETA, or retailer claims are introduced.
